How a call is contextualised

1 · Identify

The SIP From header arrives on the webhook before the call is answered, so the caller's number is known up front.

2 · Match

The number is matched against the customer records, tolerating national and international formats.

3 · Contextualise

Their profile, language and Arabic style are folded into the instructions sent when accepting the call.

4 · Converse

The agent greets the customer by name in their own language, already knowing their account context.
